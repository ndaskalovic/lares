# Privacy policy: Lares for Proxmox VE

Lares has no servers of its own and collects no telemetry or analytics.

## Stored locally, in your browser's extension storage
- Your Anthropic and/or DeepSeek API key
- Settings (provider, model, default mode, consent)
- Chat history and memory notes, per Proxmox site
- The list of Proxmox sites you enabled

This data never leaves your browser except as described below.

## Sent to the AI provider you choose
When you chat, Lares sends your messages and the Proxmox data it reads to answer them (for example VM names, configuration, logs and task output) to:
- Anthropic (`api.anthropic.com`), or
- DeepSeek (`api.deepseek.com`)

using your own API key. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies and your agreement with them.

## Proxmox
Lares talks to your Proxmox server directly from the page, using your existing login session. It never sees your Proxmox password.

## Limited use
The use of information received through this extension is limited to providing the extension's user-facing features. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to determine creditworthiness, and not transferred to anyone except the AI provider you select, as required to answer your requests.

## Deleting your data
Delete individual memory notes in Lares settings, or remove the extension to delete everything it stored.
