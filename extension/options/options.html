Privacy
Lares sends your messages, and the Proxmox data it reads to answer them (such as VM names, configs, logs and task output), to the AI provider you choose (Anthropic or DeepSeek), using your API key. Nothing else leaves your browser. Your key, chats and notes are stored only in this browser's extension storage. Lares acts with your Proxmox permissions; in Auto mode it can make changes without asking (destructive actions still ask).
